Do these first (safely)

  1. If water is near outlets, cords or the electrical panel, stay out and keep power off only if you can reach the breaker safely.
  2. Shut off the main water valve if the source is a supply line or pipe.
  3. Take photos and video before moving anything, for your insurance claim.
  4. Move valuables up and out of the water. Don't use a household vacuum on standing water.
  5. Call your homeowner's insurance to open a claim, and call a restoration provider. Drying should start within 24–48 hours to limit mold.
