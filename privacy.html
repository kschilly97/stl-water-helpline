Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This site doesn't collect forms or require accounts. When you call the number on this site, a call-tracking provider records basic call data (your phone number, the time and length of the call, and possibly a recording) so the call can be routed to an independent service provider and the referral can be measured. We share this data only with the call-routing network and the provider who receives your call. We use privacy-respecting analytics to count page visits and ad conversions, which may include Google Ads conversion tracking cookies. We do not sell personal information. We will not call or text you unless you ask us to. Questions: a contact form is coming soon.
